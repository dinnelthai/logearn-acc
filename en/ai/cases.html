Real Trading Cases

Real cases where the strategy fired, reviewed after the fact. More to be added over time. Historical cases — not investment advice.

1Wake-up Strategy Hit · LAYOOO

Flow: Wake-up strategy catches the anomaly → checking X shows the community just kicked off a CTO → don't chase the first wave, wait for a pullback to get filled → combine the signal with the event for a manual entry decision. Strategy logged roughly 10.24x.

Take: this case is a clean fit for the Wake-up Strategy's core judgment call — "dead, then revived." First check that it's been dead long enough (a long stretch of low-volume, dormant sideways action), then wait for a CTO move at the moment of revival (community takeover, restarted operations) as the confirming signal. Stacking both conditions is more reliable than jumping in on volume alone.
2Wake-up Strategy Hit · CZ26

Flow: long stretch of dead, no-volume silence → a Twitter influencer posts a meme about a "Ferrari CZ26 custom supercar" tagging @cz_binance, with the replies full of people betting on whether CZ is making a comeback → the Wake-up strategy catches the anomaly at the same time, flagging two wake-up signals → the rally confirms once the influencer's topic gains traction.

Take: same underlying logic as case 1 — dead long enough is the precondition, and an external event/topic picking it back up at the moment of revival (this time not a CTO, but a Twitter influencer meme driving attention and CZ-comeback bets) is the confirming signal. The event type can vary, but the structure — "dormant first, then re-hyped" — stays the same. The strategy fires first, the event confirms it afterward.
