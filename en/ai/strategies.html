The Three Official Strategies

The engine ships with three official strategies preloaded, all live and running with real trading data. You can treat them purely as signals, or turn on automated trading orders directly.

11.5-Stage Strategy · Strong-Market Strategy

Each strategy is one full block: a bar chart of historical hits on the left, returns and win-rate distribution in the middle, and the six-layer filter counts plus the run/pause toggle on the right.

2Wake-up Relay Strategy

This one has 自动交易 (Auto Trading) checked — once it hits, there's no manual order needed, it's placed automatically.

The Wake-up Relay Strategy is best paired with grid orders. Set the buy method to 网格挂单 (Grid Order), the reference price to 当前市值 (Current Market Cap), and the drawdown range to 30% ~ 100%. This way, a hit doesn't trigger a single market buy — instead it lays down limit orders in batches across the drawdown range from market cap, averaging down the cost basis.

Don't judge tokens-matched by the absolute number alone. A strategy with a small sample size will naturally look great on win rate — always look at total triggers and tokens matched together.

Core of the Wake-up Strategy: Sustained 1-Minute Volume

The Wake-up Relay Strategy puts heavy weight on whether 1-minute trading volume is sustained. A rising price is only the surface — whether volume underneath keeps up is the real key to deciding whether to hold or run.

Core principle: if the price rises but volume doesn't keep up, run immediately; if the first volume spike is huge but subsequent volume collapses quickly, that's also dangerous. Only rising price + sustained volume expansion is worth holding and watching.
1Sustained Volume: Price Rises, Volume Keeps Up

Volume in the red box is clearly larger than before, and the following bars stay elevated too — this kind of price/volume alignment counts as healthy.

2Danger Signal: One Huge Bar, No Follow-Through

The first volume spike is huge, but volume collapses quickly afterward — price easily loses support in this kind of move, so don't stick around.

3Healthy Trend: Volume Consistently Keeps Up with Price

Every leg of the price rise is backed by volume, which shows money is continuously flowing in — this fits the Wake-up Strategy's entry/hold criteria better.
